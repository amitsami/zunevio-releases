# Zunéviu 1.5.5

### Liquid Glass and motion
- More transparent glass for floating navigation, the mini-player and selected menus, dialogs and player controls. Adjust transparency, frost, background reflection, highlights and tint independently in Settings.
- A reflected sample of the underlying content on supported Android 13+ devices, with translucent/static fallbacks on older or low-memory devices. Menu panels and the active mini-player have subtle moving highlights.
- Three-dot menus open from and close toward their button; shared press and toggle springs have been refined. Reduced-motion and reduced-blur settings remain available.
- The Settings page keeps its existing appearance while glass controls change. The app icon is unchanged.

### Discover, search and playback
- Updated Home category selection and music shelves, with better deduplication and compact track rows where supported by the feed.
- Song recognition is accessible from Search; microphone permission and a network connection are required.
- Improved handling of selected videos from Search and artist shelves, and prevent lyrics from the previous recording appearing while a new video loads.
- Follow actions provide clearer feedback when sign-in or subscription information is unavailable. Guest listening remains available.

### About, support and compatibility
- Added an About area with version and update information and a support page. The bKash number is hidden until tapped; copying and opening bKash require an explicit action.
- Added translations for the new glass and support settings.
- The app no longer supplies an inherited Listen Together server default. The source targets Android mobile rather than the removed desktop and Wear OS targets.
- Package identity and production signing are retained. Version name is 1.5.5 and Android version code is 90.
